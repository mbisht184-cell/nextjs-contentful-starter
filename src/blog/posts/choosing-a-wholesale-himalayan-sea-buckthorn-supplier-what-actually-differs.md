\## What to Actually Compare



Sourcing model - wild-harvested vs. cultivated changes both price and authenticity story. Ask: is this wild-harvested or cultivated, and at what altitude?



Traceability - determines whether you can tell a real provenance story to your customers. Ask: can you trace this batch to a named village or grower group?



Lab documentation - sea buckthorn oil is a known adulteration target, with seed oil sometimes sold as pulp oil. Ask: can I get a CoA showing the fatty acid profile for this specific batch?



MOQ flexibility - determines whether you can test-market before committing to volume. Ask: what's your minimum for a pilot batch versus a container load?



Private label - relevant if you're building your own brand, not reselling. Ask: can you handle custom packaging and formulation?



Compliance - non-negotiable for import and export. Ask: are you FSSAI-licensed, and can you provide export documentation?

\## Frequently Asked Questions



\*\*What's the difference between wild-harvested and cultivated Himalayan sea buckthorn?\*\*

Wild-harvested sea buckthorn is hand-gathered from natural high-altitude stands, typically 2,500m and above, where cultivation isn't commercially viable. Cultivated sea buckthorn is grown on managed plantations, usually at lower altitude, and can offer more predictable year-round volume but a different, often less concentrated, phytochemical profile due to lower environmental stress.



\*\*Is a smaller, village-sourced supplier reliable for export-grade compliance?\*\*

Reliability depends on documentation, not company size - check for FSSAI licensing, batch-level Certificates of Analysis, and confirmed export track record regardless of supplier scale.
